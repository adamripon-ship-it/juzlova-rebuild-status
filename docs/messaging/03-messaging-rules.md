# 03 — Messaging rules (non-negotiable)

Derived from the product-marketing, customer-research and copywriting skills, applied to what the research in `02` actually says.

## Positioning statement (internal, never printed)

For Czech households and kitchens that cook Czech classics every week, Jůzlová is the family workshop on Vysočina whose dumpling, pudding and cocoa mixes you buy straight from the people who make them — flour from the family mill 12 km away, water-only preparation, kilo prices instead of sachet prices — unlike supermarket mixes of unknown origin sold through three middlemen.

Category shelf: *potravinářské směsi / knedlíky v prášku* (what people type). Not "poctivé české produkty" (what brands type).

## The one-sentence promise per pillar

| Pillar | Promise (must survive the "Now you can" test) |
|---|---|
| Domácnosti | Now you can put bramborové knedlíky on the table in 20 minutes, from flour you can trace, without the pot of rozvařená kejda. |
| Provozovny | Now you can serve 500 portions of potato dumplings with three people in the kitchen, with the paperwork, from a supplier who answers the phone. |
| Za hranicemi | Now you can get a box of Czech dumpling mix and pudding anywhere in the EU, and see the full shipping cost before you pay a koruna. |

## Rules

1. **Czech is the master.** Write CS first; EN/DE/SK are adaptations of the Czech *argument*, not translations of Czech *sentences*. Remove Czech-only references (Bernard brewery, Firmy.cz, "Čechy i Morava") from DE/SK/EN unless they carry meaning for that reader.
2. **One H1, one primary CTA per page.** The CTA is always the same action (call or write with what you want and where you are) with page-specific wording. Secondary CTA at most one.
3. **Say who it is for in the H1 or subhead.** Every H1 names the buyer situation (rodinný oběd, jídelna, Češi v EU), the benefit, and the proof hook.
4. **Proof before adjectives.** Allowed proof: mill 12 km away + KLASA mark (verify), since 2004, water-only prep, 15/20 minutes (one number per product), ingredient list, cocoa spec numbers from the spec sheet, delivery thresholds, lab certificate on request, reviews from live profiles. Banned adjectives: poctivý, kvalitní, prémiový, bez kompromisů, jako domácí, k nerozeznání.
5. **Never fight the "z prášku" reflex head-on.** Do not claim nobody will notice. Show the short ingredient list and invite the cook to finish it their way (egg, milk, fresh potato, parsley). This is what forum users already do; the copy should sound like them.
6. **Numbers are arithmetic, not claims.** Price per kg, per portion, per sachet-equivalent. No "4× cheaper", no "save 750 Kč" unless a named shelf price is shown.
7. **Each fact appears once per page.** Phone/address: CTA + footer only. Price: price card only. Delivery: one "Doprava" line. Disclaimers: once, under the price.
8. **Each page owns its own FAQ.** No FAQ appears on two pages. Home has no FAQ; it links to `/caste-dotazy/`.
9. **Speak to a person, not a crawler.** No sentence may mention Google, Seznam, AI, jazykové modely, "sestavení webu", or the site's build process.
10. **Voice.** Direct, warm, rural, unhurried. Short sentences. "Vy" form in CS/SK, "Sie" in DE. First person plural is the family. The owner's name may appear as the person you will speak to — that is proof, not branding.
11. **CTA labels** follow *verb + what you get*: "Zavolat Jiřině a objednat", "Napsat, co potřebujete", "Poptat cenu pro kuchyni", "Zjistit cenu zásilky do EU". Never "Odeslat", "Více", "Kontakt".
12. **Honesty items stay visible**: no e-shop; replies in Czech; no samples; price is ex-workshop; you pay EU shipping. These are objection-killers when said plainly and objection-makers when hidden.
13. **Wholesale and household never share a paragraph.** 5 kg is explained as a family stock-up on household pages and as a unit on the wholesale page.
14. **Meta title** ≤ 60 chars: keyword + place + one benefit. **Meta description** ≤ 155 chars: benefit + proof + CTA; prices allowed, never a bare list.
15. **Anything not on the live site and not confirmed by the owners goes to `06-needs-verification.md`, not into copy.**

## Words to use / avoid (from research)

| Use (customer language) | Avoid (brand language) |
|---|---|
| knedlíky v prášku, směs, těsto v prášku, "stačí přidat vodu", nerozvaří se, drží, za 20 minut, bez loupání | poctivé, kvalitní, prémiové, tradiční hodnoty, gastro řešení |
| pytlík, balení 5 kg, vydrží celou sezónu, na Vánoce | produktové portfolio, sortiment |
| kakaové máslo 20–22 %, tmavá barva, holandské kakao, do bábovky, do zmrzliny | luxusní, gurmánské, prémiová jakost |
| vanilínový cukr (say it) | vanilkový (when it is vanillin) |
| zavolejte Jiřině, vyzvednete v Kochánově, domluvíme termín | kontaktujte nás, náš tým, zákaznická podpora |
| cena dopravy předem, celá krabice dojede | logistické řešení |
