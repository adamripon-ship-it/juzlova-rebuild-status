# 02 — ICP and buyer personas (delivered before any copy)

## Method and honesty note

Jůzlová has almost no first-party review data (3 reviews on Firmy.cz, no text visible; Google profile unclaimed). Per the customer-research skill's "no reviews yet" rule I built personas outward from proxy sources, in this order: the site's own differentiators → competitor and category reviews (Amylon, Natura, Vitana mixes) → community threads where the *job* is discussed (Omlazení.cz, Mimibazar, Jídelny.cz, Kuchařka pro dceru) → press/survey data (Penny Market expat survey, Vitalia cocoa guide, Stiftung Warentest). **Every quote is verbatim from a public page and cited. Nothing is invented.** Where a source is a search-engine snippet of a page I could not open in full, it is marked *(snippet)*.

Confidence: the *jobs* and *fears* are **High** (3+ independent sources, unprompted, consistent). Demographic detail is **Low–Medium** and left blank where I have no data. Several threads are 2012–2016; they describe the job, not the current market, and are weighted accordingly. Replace these with first-party evidence as Google reviews arrive.

Three pillars, two personas each:

| Pillar | Who buys | Share of business (site's own number) |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Domácnosti** — households on Vysočina and across CZ | home cooks | ~90 % of orders are local around Kochánov |
| **2. Provozovny** — canteens, restaurants, bakeries, ice-cream makers | professional kitchens | wholesale, quantity pricing |
| **3. Za hranicemi** — Czechs living in the EU, plus SK/DE/AT buyers | expats and cross-border | parcels DE/AT/SK/PL |

## PILLAR 1 — Domácnosti (Czech households) · ICP

- Household in Vysočina / Havlíčkův Brod / Humpolec / Jihlava within a 50 km drive, or anywhere in CZ willing to pay parcel shipping.
- Cooks Czech classics weekly (knedlíky are "an integral part of Sunday lunch" — Tiscali.cz).
- Buys in bulk: a 5 kg bag is a season's supply, stored dry.
- Price-aware, additive-aware, allergic to "z prášku" as a slur, but already uses mixes when time or potato quality fails.

### Persona 1A — "Nedělní oběd bez hazardu" · the home cook who has failed at potato dumplings

**Who they are.** Woman or man 35–65, family of 3–5, rural or small-town Vysočina, cooks most days, keeps a pantry. Grew up with a grandmother's dumplings, cannot reliably reproduce them.

**Trigger event.** A batch of home-made bramborové knedlíky fell apart or came out like rubber right before guests. Or: a bad sack of potatoes. Or: someone with coeliac disease joins the family table.

**WIIFM in their words.** *"Knedlíky, které se nerozvaří, za dvacet minut, a nikdo u stolu nepozná, že jsem si pomohla."* (paraphrase of the sourced language below; not a customer quote)

**Main goals.** Sunday lunch that lands. Fruit dumplings in strawberry season without a two-hour fight. A pudding the coeliac child can eat.

**What keeps them up at night.** Cooking for people who judge. Wasting food and time. Being the one who "cheated".

**Fears (plain).** The dumplings disintegrate in the pot. The mix tastes like glue and cardboard. The family says "to je z prášku". Hidden additives they cannot pronounce. Paying for potato starch and air.

**Suspicious of.** Supermarket mixes "nejistého původu"; phrases like "poctivé" and "jako domácí" on every pack; savings figures that seem made up; a business with no e-shop (is it real? will they answer?).

**The enemy.** The pot of rozvařená kejda. Unreliable potatoes. Big-brand mixes at 4× the price per kg. The afternoon lost to peeling and grating.

**Objections and counters.**

| Objection | Counter (only provable facts) |
|---|---|
| "Z prášku to nikdy nechutná jako doma." | Flour from the family mill 12 km away with the KLASA mark, dried potato flakes, water. You add the egg, the fresh potato, the parsley if you want; forum cooks already do this with mixes. |
| "5 kg je moc." | 5 kg = about 60–70 side portions; stores dry; one bag covers a season of Sundays. 400 g pudding exists for a first try. |
| "Musím jet do Kochánova?" | Free pickup in Kochánov or Humpolec, free delivery to Humpolec/HB from 1000 Kč, Jihlava from 3000 Kč, Vysočina from 5000 Kč, parcel anywhere in CZ. |
| "Není tam e-shop, je to vůbec seriózní?" | You phone Jiřina, who mixes it, daily 8–19. Company ID, address, 20 years, live Google/Firmy.cz reviews. |

### Persona 1B — "Peču pro rodinu a na Vánoce" · the home baker who buys pudding, cocoa and vanilla sugar by the kilo

**Who they are.** Woman 30–60, bakes weekly and heavily before Christmas and Easter; makes věnečky, větrníky, perník, bebe řezy; often bakes for a school fair or extended family. Reads dTest/Vitalia-type comparisons.

**Trigger event.** Christmas baking list. A cocoa that baked out pale and bitter. A coeliac relative. Running out of 40 g sachets and doing the maths.

**WIIFM.** *"Kakao, co dá bábovce tmavou barvu a chutná po čokoládě, ne po prachu — a puding, který dostanu v kile, ne v sáčcích za třicet korun."*

**Main goals.** Dark, moist cakes that look like the picture. Cream that sets. Fewer trips to the shop in December. Not paying sachet prices.

**Keeps them up at night.** A tray of perník that came out grey. Guests with allergies. Waste.

**Fears.** Cheap cocoa that is "obarvený prach". Fat-reduced cocoa sold as cocoa. Pudding that tastes artificial. A kilo of sugar going hard in the pantry.

**Suspicious of.** "Vanilla" that is vanillin (they want it said plainly). Nutrition tables with round numbers. Cocoa origin stories without a certificate.

**The enemy.** 10 % fat supermarket cocoa. Sachet pricing. Dry cake.

**Objections and counters.**

| Objection | Counter |
|---|---|
| "270 Kč za 500 g kakaa je hodně." | Per gram it is a 20–22 % cocoa-butter confectionery cocoa; fat-reduced supermarket cocoa is a different product (legal threshold 20 %, Vitalia). Show price per 10 g serving. |
| "Je to pravá vanilka?" | No. It is vanillin sugar, said in the product name. |
| "Puding v kile zvlhne." | Corn starch stores dry for months; 400 g pack exists. |
| "Nevím, kolik pudinku dát místo sáčku." | A sachet is about 40 g. One kilo = 25 sachets. Print this. |

**Exact words (sourced).**

- *"Čím více kakaového másla ve výrobku, tím vyšší cena i kvalita."* — Kvinta (Dr. Oetker), quoted in Kateřina Čepelíková, "Nejlepší kakao do cukroví: i na barvě záleží", Vitalia.cz, 7 Dec 2020, https://www.vitalia.cz/clanky/nejlepsi-kakao-do-cukrovi-na-barve-zalezi/
- *"Nejraději používám holandské kakao, zejména pak pro výraznou chuť a také krásnou tmavou barvu."* — mistr cukrář Maršálek, same article
- *"V 99 % případů nezáleží na tom, jaký typ použijete. Výsledným rozdílem bude pouze barva"* — Maršálek, same article (a counter-voice: colour is the visible differentiator)
- Search snippet from the same coverage: cheap cocoa *"jsou v podstatě jen 'odpad' po vylisování másla, který moc nevoní a chutná spíš jako obarvený prach"* *(snippet; wording as surfaced by search, attributable to Vitalia/iReceptář coverage of Dutch cocoa)*
- Legal frame buyers cite: *"Podle vyhlášky č. 76/2003 Sb. musí kakaový prášek obsahovat nejméně 20 % kakaového másla"* *(snippet, Vitalia.cz)*
- Amylon gluten-free vanilla pudding is rated *"4,3 z 5"* on Heureka with users praising *"bez palmového oleje a bez přidaného cukru"* *(snippet, https://pudingy.heureka.cz/amylon-bio-puding-vanilkovy-40g/)* — shows what the pudding buyer checks first.

**Implication for copy.** Talk in fat %, colour, and price per sachet-equivalent. State vanillin honestly. Keep the cocoa spec table (it is the proof), cut the invented flavour scores.

## PILLAR 2 — Provozovny (professional kitchens) · ICP

- School canteens (školní jídelny), company canteens, village restaurants, pubs with a lunch menu within delivery range; bakeries/cukrárny and ice-cream makers anywhere in CZ for cocoa.
- Decision maker: vedoucí školní jídelny / hlavní kuchař(ka) / owner-chef. Buys on: reliability per 100 portions, labour hours, price per portion, ingredient paperwork (allergens, certificates), and whether the director will approve a "polotovar".
- Orders 25 kg+ mixed, wants a delivery slot, wants a lab certificate for cocoa batches.

### Persona 2A — "Tisíc porcí, tři lidi" · the school-canteen head cook

**Who they are.** Vedoucí kuchařka, 40–60, 3–4 staff, 300–1000 diners a day, works to spotřební koš norms and a per-portion budget, answers to a ředitelka who may frown on "polotovary".

**Trigger event.** Potato quality collapsed ("kvalita brambor je dneska na nízké úrovni"). A fruit-dumpling day with 500 diners. Staff shortage. A supplier stopped delivering.

**WIIFM.** *"Bramborové knedlíky pro celou školu bez loupání třiceti kilo brambor, které vyjdou na cenu, a papíry k tomu."*

**Goals.** Portions out on time. Cost per portion inside the norm. Consistent texture so kids eat it. Documentation for hygiene audits.

**Keeps them up at night.** Being told they cannot use the mix they already bought. A batch that doesn't hold. Allergen questions from parents.

**Fears.** The director banning polotovary. Kids leaving it on the plate. Supplier who doesn't pick up the phone on a Monday morning.

**Suspicious of.** Big distributors' minimum orders and price lists that change without notice; "premium" claims with no spec sheet; a family firm that cannot deliver at scale.

**The enemy.** 30 kg of bad potatoes. The norm spreadsheet. A workday that starts at 5 a.m. peeling.

**Objections and counters.**

| Objection | Counter |
|---|---|
| "Ředitelka nechce polotovary." | Ingredient list is flour + potato flakes; cook adds water; you can add fresh potato. Give her the label and the KLASA flour source. |
| "Potřebuji 25 kg každý týden, zvládnete to?" | Yes: workshop pickup, own delivery, or your carrier; Prague from 25 kg mixed; Brno free from 5000 Kč. Say lead time — **Verify with owners**. |
| "Cena?" | Quantity price by phone; publish at least "5 kg retail 250 Kč, wholesale lower" so they know it's worth calling. |
| "Alergeny a certifikáty?" | Cocoa: none of the 14 EU allergens, gluten < 20 ppm, lab certificate per batch on request. Dumpling mixes: wheat (gluten) declared. |

**Exact words (sourced, Jídelny.cz thread "Bramborové knedlíky", https://jidelny.cz/diskuze/bramborove-knedliky/).**

- *"Bramborové knedlíky ze směsi jsem si vymohla, nakonec zjistili, že jsou výborné"* — věrka, 27 Feb 2012
- *"Od čeho máme směs na bramborové knedlíky, když je nesmíme používat?"* — Evča 1, 27 Feb 2012
- *"uvařit dvacet, třicet kilo brambor, oloupat je… trvá mnohem déle"* — Evča 1, 27 Feb 2012
- *"Kvalita brambor je dneska na nízké úrovni… raději zvolím směs, která je bezproblémová"* — eva 2, 1 Mar 2012
- *"cena používaných dalších surovin je celkem vysoká"* — eva 2, 1 Mar 2012
- Related Jídelny.cz threads *(snippets)*: *"jako jediný polotovar používá těsto na bramborové knedlíky"*; *"ovocné knedlíky pro 500 strávníků v počtu 3-4 osob v kuchyni"*; *"kupování špaldových knedlíků v prášku nebo jako polotovar je vyhazování peněz"* (the price-sensitivity counter-voice) — https://jidelny.cz/diskuze/spaldove-knedliky/ and related

**Implication.** Wholesale page must speak labour, portions, paperwork, delivery certainty. "Cena podle množství" alone is not a reason to call.

### Persona 2B — "Hosté poznají prášek" · the small restaurant / cukrárna / ice-cream owner

**Who they are.** Owner-operator, 30–55, village or small-town restaurant with a daily menu, or a cukrárna / zmrzlinář buying cocoa. Reputation-driven; reads own Google reviews.

**Trigger event.** A guest review saying the dumplings were "z prášku". A cocoa that faded in the ice cream. Rising staff costs.

**WIIFM.** *"Přílohu a dezert, na kterých hosté nepoznají úsporu, a dodavatele, který zvedne telefon."*

**Goals.** Consistent plate every day. Margin. A cocoa that stays dark in gelato and glaze. No stock-outs.

**Keeps them up at night.** Bad reviews. Food cost. Staff turnover.

**Fears.** Being caught serving a polotovar as "domácí". Grey ice cream. A supplier who vanishes in summer.

**Suspicious of.** "Restaurant quality" marketing; suppliers who won't give a spec sheet; hidden delivery charges.

**The enemy.** The guest who "knows". Food-cost spreadsheets. Fat-reduced cocoa.

**Objections and counters.**

| Objection | Counter |
|---|---|
| "Hosté poznají směs." | Don't claim they won't. Sell the base: KLASA flour + potato flakes, cook finishes with fresh potato/egg/herbs; the plate is theirs. |
| "Kakao do zmrzliny musí držet barvu." | 20–22 % cocoa butter, medium alkalisation pH 7.8–8.2, deep red-brown; the site already states this; supply the spec sheet. |
| "Doprava mi to prodraží." | Own carrier accepted; free Brno from 5000 Kč; Vysočina from 5000 Kč. |

**Exact words (sourced).**

- *"dost z nich křičí, že jsou z prášku"* — guest complaint about restaurant dumplings claimed as home-made, in "Práškové polotovary v našich restauracích", Vaření.cz magazine, https://www.vareni.cz/magazin/praskove-polotovary-v-nasich-restauracich/ *(snippet; article body not retrievable)*
- *"stačí přidat vodu a jídlo je hotové"* — same article *(snippet)*
- *"Kvalita knedlíků ze směsí je dnes na vysoké úrovni, pokud si vybere správného výrobce"* — chef discussion as surfaced in search for the same topic *(snippet)*
- *"Sousedé se v restauraci rozplývali nad domácí omáčkou za 200 Kč. Kuchař se jim smál, vytáhl ji totiž jako polotovar z mrazáku"* — TVGURU.cz headline, https://www.tvguru.cz/slabiny-ceske-gastronomie/ (the reputational fear, verbatim headline)
- *"Kuchař z luxusní restaurace mi přiznal, co dávají do jídla. Od té doby tam už nejím"* — Techsvět headline, https://techsvet.cz/domacnost/kuchar-z-luxusni-restaurace-mi-priznal-co-davaji-do-jidla-od-te-doby-tam-uz-nejim/karolmichalikova/

**Implication.** Never promise "guests won't tell". Promise base quality, spec sheets, colour that holds, and a phone that is answered.

## PILLAR 3 — Za hranicemi (Czechs in the EU; SK / DE / AT buyers) · ICP

- Czech and Slovak nationals living in DE / AT / SK / PL / other EU, who cannot buy Czech-style dumplings or Czech flour locally; occasionally German/Austrian home cooks near the border who like Böhmische Knödel.
- Buys a box of shelf-stable mixes 2–4× a year, expects to pay shipping, wants the total before paying, wants it to survive the parcel.

### Persona 3A — "Chybí mi knedlíky" · the Czech living in Germany/Austria/UK-adjacent EU

**Who they are.** 25–50, works abroad, partner may be non-Czech, cooks Czech food for homesickness and to show the kids. Orders Czech goods in bulk, shares tips in expat Facebook groups.

**Trigger event.** Christmas/Easter approaching. A failed attempt to make knedlíky from German flour. Visiting family who bring a box.

**WIIFM.** *"Krabici českých knedlíků a pudingu, která dojede celá, s cenou dopravy předem, bez toho, abych prosila rodiče o pašování."*

**Goals.** Sunday Czech lunch abroad. Kids who know what svíčková is. A stock that lasts until the next visit home.

**Keeps them up at night.** Homesickness at the table. Parcel broken or lost. Customs confusion (none inside EU, but they don't know).

**Fears.** Paying shipping and then finding out the price. Powder bags bursting in transit. The seller not replying in a language they can use.

**Suspicious of.** Any Czech e-shop with vague shipping; sellers who don't say "you pay X". Unlabelled fees.

**The enemy.** German Kartoffelknödel ("hutná koule"). German flour that "doesn't work". The 600 km to the nearest babička.

**Objections and counters.**

| Objection | Counter |
|---|---|
| "Kolik mě to bude stát celkem?" | Goods at list price + tracked shipping + box + insurance + bubble wrap; you get the total before anything ships (already the site's policy — make it the headline). |
| "Přijde to celé?" | Say how it is packed (bubble wrap, box, insured). |
| "Odpovíte mi německy/anglicky?" | Replies are in Czech; owners use a translator for German. Say it plainly, once. |
| "Mohu poslat rodičům do Česka místo toho?" | Yes: delivery anywhere in CZ, pickup Kochánov/Humpolec. Offer the "gift to family in CZ" route. |

**Exact words (sourced).**

- *"Zhruba čtvrtina respondentů si zase posteskne po domácích knedlíkech."* — Penny Market survey reported by ePeníze.eu, 31 Jul 2024, https://www.epenize.eu/po-cem-se-nam-v-zahranici-nejvice-styska-prvni-misto-neprekvapi/
- *"Typické české bramborové i ty, které se je třeba nechat kynout."* — same article
- *"55 % respondentů chybí v zahraničí čerstvý bochník chleba."* — same article (bread first, dumplings second: the wider "Czech pantry" job)
- *"V Německu si nekoupíte rohlík, hladkou mouku ani knedlík"* — Médium.cz (Seznam) headline, https://medium.seznam.cz/clanek/anylan-v-nemecku-si-nekoupite-rohlik-hladkou-mouku-kvalitni-knedlik-zato-se-zde-legalne-zhulite-187574 ; body *(snippet)*: Czechs abroad *"pašují mouku z domova"*, and the German Kartoffelknödel is *"hutná koule, která má s českým houskovým knedlíkem společný jen název"*
- Shipping-forum framing *(snippet, balikdozahranici.cz FAQ)*: within the EU you may send food *"pouze trvanlivé potraviny, které se nekazí"*

**Implication.** The EU page should be written as a *total-cost-first* checklist with packing detail and the "gift to family in CZ" option, not as a legal disclaimer.

### Persona 3B — "Böhmische Knödel, aber richtig" · the German/Austrian or Slovak border buyer

**Who they are.** Home cook in Bavaria, Saxony, Upper/Lower Austria or western Slovakia within a few hours' drive; has eaten Czech dumplings on holiday or has Czech in-laws. Slovak buyers additionally want halušky/strapačky base.

**Trigger event.** Holiday in Šumava/Vysočina; a Stiftung Warentest reading that "self-made still tastes best"; Slovak buyer's grandmother's strapačky.

**WIIFM.** *"Ein Knödelteig, der wirklich böhmisch schmeckt und nicht breiig wird — und den ich in der Nähe abholen oder geliefert bekomme."*

**Goals.** Authentic result without a Czech grandmother. Reasonable cost incl. shipping. Clear instructions in their language.

**Keeps them up at night.** Nothing about this; low-stakes. Fear is mild: waste of money on a foreign product they can't return.

**Fears.** Mushy texture ("breiiges Mundgefühl"). Instructions only in Czech. Paying in a foreign currency with unclear shipping.

**Suspicious of.** Cheap Fertigmischungen with "Antioxidationsmittel, Aroma"; foreign sellers without an Impressum-style identity block.

**The enemy.** Pfanni-style Fertigknödel. The dense German Kloß when they want a light Czech one.

**Objections and counters.**

| Objection | Counter |
|---|---|
| "Wie selbstgemacht?" | Do not claim it. Say ingredients (Weizenmehl KLASA, Kartoffelflocken), 15–20 min, add egg/potato if you like. |
| "Versand nach Deutschland?" | Yes: DE/AT/SK/PL parcels, you pay shipping, cost quoted first, prices in Kč with € indication. |
| "Wer seid ihr?" | Family workshop since 2004, company ID, address, phone; owners reply in Czech, sometimes with a translator. |

**Exact words (sourced).**

- *"Selbstgemachte Kartoffelklöße schmecken immer noch am besten, doch einige Klöße aus dem Test kommen ihm recht nahe."* — Stiftung Warentest, "Kartoffelklöße im Test", https://www.test.de/Kartoffelkloesse-im-Test-4148740-0/ *(snippet)*
- *"Bei einigen Produkten trübt ein breiiges Mundgefühl den Genuss."* — same *(snippet)*
- Typical mix ingredient list German buyers scan: *"Kartoffelstärke, Salz, Antioxidationsmittel, Gewürze oder Aroma"* — same coverage *(snippet)*; Jůzlová's list is shorter, which is the message.
- Slovak framing: *"Bryndzové halušky… chewy potato dumplings"* — Lonely Planet, https://www.lonelyplanet.com/articles/what-to-eat-and-drink-in-slovakia (job: halušky/strapačky base)

**Implication.** DE copy must be Sachlich: ingredient list, time, price incl. shipping logic, identity block. SK copy must lead with strapačky/halušky, not "knedle".
