Potato dough powder is our most sought-after product. We use quality wheat flour awarded the KLASA mark from a nearby mill in Havlíčkův Brod; dried potato flakes provide the typical taste and texture.

What you can make from the dough

  • classic potato dumplings for roast meat
  • dumplings filled with meat or fruit
  • poppy-seed rolls (šišky s mákem)
  • deep-fried croquettes
  • homemade gnocchi

The natural taste tells you there’s nothing extra in the dough — only what belongs in a potato dumpling.

How long does preparation take?

Just mix the powder with water, shape and boil — dumplings are on the table within 20 minutes.

How many servings per 5 kg bag?

One 5 kg bag makes a side dish for roughly 60–70 servings.
