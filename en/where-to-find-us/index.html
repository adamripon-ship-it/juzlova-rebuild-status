Pick-up in Humpolec

A second pick-up point is in Humpolec near the Bernard brewery, free of charge. We give the exact spot, day and time by phone.

Delivery and parcels

  • Humpolec and Havlíčkův Brod: free delivery from 1000 CZK
  • Jihlava: free from 3000 CZK
  • Rest of Vysočina: free from 5000 CZK
  • Elsewhere in Czechia: parcel, shipping quoted first; you may also send your own courier
  • Germany, Austria, Slovakia, Poland and the rest of the EU: you pay shipping, box and insurance; you see the total before we ship
