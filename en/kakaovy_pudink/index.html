Gluten-free cocoa pudding

Ready in five minutes. Light dessert, diet creams or baking — with Dutch-process cocoa.

Price

How to order
Contact · +420 728 466 141 · juzlj@seznam.cz

Our cocoa pudding is ready in five minutes. It makes a light dessert, a base for diet creams, or a baking ingredient.

The base is gluten-free corn starch. We blend in the same Dutch-process cocoa with 21% fat that we sell on its own.

How to cook it

Set aside a third of 500 ml of milk and stir in 40 g of sugar and 40 g of pudding. Bring the rest of the milk to a boil. Add the mixed slurry and cook, stirring, for another 2 minutes.

Is the pudding gluten-free?

Yes. The base is pure corn starch.

How is it different from the vanilla?

The cocoa version uses Dutch-process cocoa with 21% fat — a pronounced chocolate taste.
