Bebe biscuit slices with chocolate pudding

Want to treat your guests but you're no Jamie Oliver? These no-bake biscuit slices are for everyone.

Mix used
Dutch-process cocoa

Ingredients

  • bebe biscuits (butter biscuits)
  • Jůzlová cocoa pudding powder
  • milk and sugar per the pudding instructions
  • butter
  • whipping cream

Method

  1. Line the bottom of a dish with a layer of biscuits.
  2. Cook a thicker cocoa pudding, let it cool slightly and whisk in the butter.
  3. Spread the cream over the biscuits, top with another biscuit layer and chill until set.
  4. Decorate with whipped cream before serving.
