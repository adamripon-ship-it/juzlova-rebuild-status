How it works

  1. Tell us what you want and where it should go.

How we pack

Bags in bubble wrap, in a sturdy box, insured and tracked. The mixes keep for months — until your next trip home.

Frequently asked questions

Is there customs duty?

Not inside the EU. You pay the goods, shipping, box, insurance and bubble wrap.

Where do you ship most?

Germany, Austria, Slovakia and Poland. Elsewhere in the EU we quote shipping.

Do you reply in English?

We reply in Czech; for English and German we sometimes use a translation tool.
