Quick choux wreaths or puffs

One choux pastry for both věnečky and větrníčky: extra butter and a 250 °C start. About 80 pieces on two trays.

Rate this recipe

/ 5 ·

Ingredients

  • 250 ml water
  • a pinch of salt
  • 90 g butter
  • 140 g coarse flour (hrubá mouka)
  • 4 eggs
  • fat, for greasing the trays

Method

  1. Bring the water, salt and butter to a boil.
  2. Reduce the heat and stir in the flour all at once; mix until the dough pulls cleanly away from the pan and is no longer sticky.
  3. Take it off the heat and let it cool slightly.
  4. Preheat the oven to 250 °C.
  5. Beat in the eggs one at a time, mixing continuously, until the batter is smooth and slightly elastic.
  6. Pipe onto the greased trays: two stacked rings for věnečky, or small round mounds for větrníčky.
  7. Bake at 250 °C for 5 minutes, then reduce to 200 °C and bake until golden — roughly 5 more minutes at the higher heat, then 5 at the lower. Do not open the oven door while baking.
  8. Slice them straight away while still hot (this stops the shells tearing later). Cool completely before filling.

The filling is up to you: sweetened whipped cream is the simplest. For věnečky use the vanilla buttercream and rum glaze from the wreath recipe; for větrníčky use the vanilla pudding cream, caramel whipped cream and caramel glaze from the caramel větrník recipe.

This dough uses a higher butter-to-flour ratio (90 g : 140 g) and a hot 250 °C start, which is why it puffs faster and more reliably.

Frequently asked questions

Why bake cream puffs at 250 °C?

A hot start at 250 °C and a higher butter ratio (90 g to 140 g flour) make the choux rise fast. After 5 minutes drop to 200 °C. About 80 pieces on two trays.

Can one dough make both rings and puffs?

Yes. The same choux: pipe rings as two stacked circles and puffs as small mounds. Fill with whipped cream, vanilla custard or caramel as you like.
