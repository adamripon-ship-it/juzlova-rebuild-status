What it goes with

  • sauerkraut and smoked meat
  • fried onion and cracklings
  • sweet, with poppy seeds and sugar
  • pushed through a colander into boiling water as halušky and strapačky

How to make it

  1. Mix with water as the bag says.
  2. Shape bosáky, or push the dough through a colander.
  3. Boil. 15 minutes from bag to plate.

For canteens

We supply bosáky to canteens and restaurants too: the same mix, quantity pricing, pick-up in Kochánov, our delivery, or your carrier.

Frequently asked questions

Are chlupaté knedlíky and bosáky the same thing?

Yes, two names for one dish.

Can I make halušky from it?

Yes; push the dough through a colander into boiling water. The strapačky recipe is on the site.

Is there a pack smaller than 5 kg?

No. 5 kg is the only pack.
