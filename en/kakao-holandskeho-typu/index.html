Our dark confectionery cocoa is Dutch-process — an alkalised powder from Theobroma cacao beans, 21% fat class. Full cocoa body, a creamy chocolate taste and a deep red-brown colour that holds both dry and in the finished recipe.

Where the cocoa comes from

The beans dry slowly in the sun in Central West Africa. They ferment naturally in banana leaves — that is how the deep chocolate flavour is born, which we then soften by alkalisation into Dutch-process cocoa.

Fat 20–22%: why the range matters

We keep cocoa butter at 20.0–22.0%. That is more fat than in lean supermarket cocoas (often 10–12%). More butter means more moisture in the dough, a rounder mouthfeel and a fuller chocolate impression — the 21% class pastry cooks look for.

No added sugar

We add no sugar. The only sugars you will find — 0.9 g per 100 g — occur naturally in the cocoa bean.

Full-bodied cocoa intensity, a creamy chocolate flavour and roasted notes. The colour is a deep red-brown — in the powder and in the finished cake.

Where it belongs

Frequently asked questions

What makes Dutch-process cocoa different?

Alkalisation — it is darker, less acidic and dissolves better than natural cocoa. At 20–22% fat it ranks among the finest confectionery cocoas.

Do you add sugar?

No. Added sugar is 0 g. Naturally occurring sugars from the bean are 0.9 g per 100 g.

Is it gluten-free and allergen-free?

Yes. It contains none of the 14 EU allergens. Gluten is below 20 ppm. It is vegan and vegetarian — no ingredients of animal origin.

How should I store it?

Dry, away from strong odours, ideally at 15–20 °C with humidity under 50%.
