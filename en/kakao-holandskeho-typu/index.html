Dutch-process cocoa

We offer an excellent dark confectionery cocoa of the Dutch type with 21% fat.

It stands out in cakes, giving them a rich, dark cocoa colour and flavour. It also makes delicious milkshakes and creams. The same cocoa goes into our cocoa pudding.

What makes Dutch-process cocoa different?

Alkalisation — it is darker, less acidic and dissolves better than natural cocoa. At 21% fat it ranks among the finest confectionery cocoas.
