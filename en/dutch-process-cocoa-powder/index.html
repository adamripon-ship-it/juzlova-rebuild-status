Why the fat matters

Under EU law a powder needs at least 20 % cocoa butter to be called cocoa; below that it is fat-reduced cocoa. Ours has 20–22 %. More cocoa butter means a darker colour, a fuller taste and a smoother cream.

Where it comes from

Beans from West-Central Africa, fermented in banana leaves and sun-dried. We alkalise with natural salts to pH 7.8–8.2 — hence the deep red-brown colour and a rounded, not sour, taste.

What is not in it

No added sugar (0.9 g of natural sugars per 100 g). None of the 14 EU allergens, gluten under 20 ppm. Vegan.

Where to use it

  • Baking

    Gingerbread, bundt cake, brownies — batters that should stay deep cocoa after baking.

  • Drinks

    Hot cocoa and shakes — it keeps its colour and creamy taste in milk.

  • Creams

    Fillings and creams without added sugar in the powder.

  • Dusting

    Slices, roulades and biscuit desserts — a dark, fine dust that does not clump.

  • With vanilla pudding

    Stir into our vanilla pudding: chocolate cream from two mixes.

  • Ice cream

    Smooth texture and a dark colour that does not fade in the freezer.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the cocoa price change?

Cocoa is a traded commodity: weather, demand and new EU heavy-metal limits move the price. We adjust the list when our purchase price changes.

Is it raw cocoa?

No. It is alkalised (Dutch-process) and roasted. Raw cocoa is unroasted and tastes bitter and grassy; ours is smooth and rounded with roasted notes.

Does it work in ice cream?

Yes. The higher cocoa-butter content gives ice cream a smooth texture and a dark colour that does not fade in the freezer.
