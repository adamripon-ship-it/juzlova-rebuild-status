Delivery

Our workshop is in Kochánov, 12 km from Havlíčkův Brod. We’re available every day from 8:00 to 19:00, weekends included.

We know that alongside quality, price matters to our customers. Because carrier fees can make our products more expensive, we offer pick-up directly at our premises in Kochánov. Just let us know in advance and we’ll have your order ready any time.

By prior arrangement you can also pick up your order in Humpolec. We currently deliver in and around Havlíčkův Brod, Humpolec, Světlá nad Sázavou and Jihlava — and by individual arrangement we’ll bring the goods right to your door.

Delivery options

  1. Pick-up at our premises in Kochánov or in Humpolec — free
  2. Free delivery around Havlíčkův Brod, Světlá nad Sázavou and Jihlava
  3. A carrier of your choice (shipping per the carrier’s price list)

When to stop by

Open daily by phone arrangement: Monday–Saturday, 8:00–19:00.
