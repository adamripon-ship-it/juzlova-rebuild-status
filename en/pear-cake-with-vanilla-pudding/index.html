Pear cake with vanilla pudding

A cake your children will enjoy making with you. The fun comes twice: once in the making, once in the eating.

Rate this recipe

/ 5 ·

Mix used
Gluten-free vanilla pudding powder

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 80 g melted butter
  • 150 g granulated sugar
  • 200 g plain flour (can be mixed with wholemeal or spelt)
  • 12 g baking powder (about 1 tablespoon)
  • 2 cartons sour cream
  • 1 carton whipping cream (30 %)
  • 40 g vanilla pudding powder
  • 5 pears (or apples, raspberries or peaches, fresh or frozen)

Method

  1. Mix one egg, the butter, 50 g of the sugar, the flour and the baking powder. Work the dough by hand and press it into a cake tin lined with baking paper (a crumbly dough is fine; just press it in).
  2. Peel the pears, slice them and spread over the dough.
  3. In a bowl, combine the sour cream, whipping cream, pudding powder, the remaining 100 g of sugar and the second egg. Pour it over the pears.
  4. Bake in the middle of an oven preheated to 180 °C for about 45 minutes.

Frequently asked questions

How long does the pear and vanilla-pudding cake bake?

20 minutes prep and about 45 minutes at 180 °C. The recipe serves 8 and also works with apples, raspberries or peaches.

How much pudding mix goes into the pear cake?

40 g of our vanilla pudding powder in the poured layer with sour cream, whipping cream, sugar and egg.
