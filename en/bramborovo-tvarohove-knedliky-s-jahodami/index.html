Potato-and-curd dumplings with strawberries

Summer is here, and with it ripening strawberries. A simple 20-minute recipe that delights the sweetest tooth.

Mix used
Potato dumpling mix

Ingredients

  • Jůzlová potato dough (per pack instructions)
  • curd cheese (tvaroh)
  • fresh strawberries
  • icing sugar, melted butter

Method

  1. Work the curd cheese into the potato dough.
  2. Flatten pieces of dough, wrap a strawberry in each and shape into dumplings.
  3. Boil in salted water until the dumplings float.
  4. Serve dusted with sugar and curd, drizzled with melted butter.
