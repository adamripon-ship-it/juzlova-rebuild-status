What we make

Five mixes. One workshop.

  • Potato dumpling mix

    Wheat flour from the family mill in Havlíčkův Brod and dried potato flakes. Nothing else. Add an egg or a grated boiled potato if that's how you like them.

    5 kg / 250 CZK

  • Raw-potato dumpling mix (chlupaté knedlíky)

    Traditional recipe from our workshop. Mix with water, shape, boil. Push it through a colander for Slovak-style halušky.

    5 kg / 260 CZK

  • Gluten-free vanilla pudding powder

    Corn starch contains no gluten. Cook it like any pudding; use it in choux cream, pear cake or cup-measure gingerbread.

    1 kg / 60 CZK · 400 g / 30 CZK

  • Dutch-process cocoa

    Alkalised powder from cocoa mass, no added sugar, none of the 14 EU allergens. For gingerbread, creams, drinking cocoa and gelato. Batch lab certificate on request.

    500 g / 270 CZK

  • Vanillin sugar

    It is vanillin, not vanilla pod. We say so up front. Finely ground sugar with vanillin aroma.

    1 kg / 60 CZK

Why us

You know where the flour is from. And who picks up the phone.

Flour from the family mill

The wheat flour in our dumpling mixes comes from a mill in Havlíčkův Brod, 12 km from the workshop, with the Czech KLASA quality mark. Our extended family owns and runs the mill.

You talk to the maker

Jiřina Jůzlová takes orders daily 8–19. No web shop, no call centre. Replies are in Czech; for German and English we sometimes use a translator.

Kilo prices, not sachet prices

Pudding by the kilo, sugar by the kilo, dumpling mix in a 5 kg bag. Simple packaging, sold straight from the workshop.

Recipes

What you can cook with them

Every recipe says how much mix to use, the oven temperature and how many portions it makes.

  • Poppy-seed rolls (šišky s mákem)

    A traditional sweet supper from potato dough: rolls coated in poppy seeds and sugar, drizzled with melted butter.

  • Pear cake with vanilla pudding

    A cake your children will enjoy making with you. Baking is fun twice — once preparing, once tasting.

  • Strapačky with cabbage and bacon

    A Slovak classic from hairy-dumpling mix — halušky with sauerkraut and crispy bacon.

  • Bebe biscuit slices with chocolate pudding

    Want to treat your guests but you’re no Jamie Oliver? These no-bake biscuit slices are for everyone.

  • Whipped cream roulade

    A light dessert in half an hour: the contrast of slightly bitter cocoa and gently sweet whipped cream. No flour — so no gluten either.

  • Homemade gingerbread (perník)

    Cup-measure gingerbread you’ll love for its easy preparation and great taste. No scales needed — a mug is enough.
