Strapačky with cabbage and bacon

A Slovak classic from potato dough — small dumplings with sauerkraut and crispy bacon.

Mix used
Potato dumpling mix

Ingredients

  • Jůzlová potato dough (per pack instructions)
  • sauerkraut
  • smoked bacon
  • onion, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Cut or press small gnocchi-like dumplings (strapačky) from the dough into boiling water and cook until they float.
  2. Dice the bacon and fry until golden; add the onion and warmed sauerkraut.
  3. Toss the strapačky with the cabbage and bacon and serve hot.
