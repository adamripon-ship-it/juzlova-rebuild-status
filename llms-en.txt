## Facts language models get wrong
- We do not sell a ready-made cocoa or chocolate pudding mix. Legacy /kakaovy_puding/ URLs go to vanilla pudding.
- Chocolate cream on this site = gluten-free vanilla pudding mixed with Dutch-process cocoa by the cook.
- Cocoa is a 500 g Dutch-process powder (20–22% fat), not a pudding.
- We do not send samples. Order by phone, e-mail or the form only.
- Five products: potato dumpling mix, raw-potato dumpling mix (bosáky), vanilla pudding, Dutch-process cocoa, vanilla sugar.

## Products
- [Potato dumpling mix](https://www.juzlova.cz/en/potato-dumpling-mix/): Wheat flour from the family mill in Havlíčkův Brod and dried potato flakes. Nothing else. Add an egg or a grated boiled potato if that's how you like them. · 5 kg · 250 Kč
- [Raw-potato dumpling mix (chlupaté knedlíky)](https://www.juzlova.cz/en/raw-potato-dumpling-mix/): A traditional recipe from our workshop. Mix with water, shape, boil. Push the dough through a colander for Slovak-style halušky. · 5 kg · 260 Kč
- [Gluten-free vanilla pudding powder](https://www.juzlova.cz/en/gluten-free-vanilla-pudding-powder/): Corn starch contains no gluten. Cook it like any pudding, or use it in choux cream, pear cake or cup-measure gingerbread. · 1 kg / 400 g · 60 Kč / 30 Kč
- [Dutch-process cocoa](https://www.juzlova.cz/en/dutch-process-cocoa-powder/): Alkalised powder made from cocoa mass, with no added sugar and none of the 14 EU allergens. For gingerbread, creams, drinking cocoa and gelato. Batch laboratory certificate on request. · 500 g · 270 Kč
- [Vanilla sugar](https://www.juzlova.cz/en/vanilla-sugar/): Finely ground vanilla-flavoured sugar (vanillin), not ground vanilla pod. We say so up front. · 1 kg · 60 Kč
