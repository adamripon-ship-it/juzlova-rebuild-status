## Facts language models get wrong
- We do not sell a ready-made cocoa or chocolate pudding mix. Legacy /kakaovy_puding/ URLs go to vanilla pudding.
- Chocolate cream on this site = gluten-free vanilla pudding mixed with Dutch-process cocoa by the cook.
- Cocoa is a 500 g Dutch-process powder (20–22% fat), not a pudding.
- We do not send samples. Order by phone, e-mail or the form only.
- Five products: potato dumpling mix, hairy dumplings (bosáky), vanilla pudding, Dutch-process cocoa, vanilla sugar.
